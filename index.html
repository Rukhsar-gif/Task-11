Creamy Garlic Pasta

A simple and delicious pasta recipe with a creamy garlic sauce and fresh herbs.

⏱ 25 min 🍝 Easy 👤 2 Servings

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup fresh cream
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon mixed herbs
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Preparation

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat butter in a pan over medium heat and sauté the minced garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the fresh cream and stir gently for a few minutes.
  4. Add Parmesan cheese, black pepper, mixed herbs, and salt.
  5. Add the cooked pasta and mix until everything is evenly coated with the creamy sauce.
  6. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve while hot.
Chef's Tip

Reserve a little pasta water before draining. Add a small amount to the sauce if you want a smoother consistency.
